# Geographic and community evidence ledger

Date: 2026-10-24. Companion to the [research report and source register](README.md).

Re-audit: see [corrections and acceptance criteria](audit-and-realism.md). This ledger tracks population evidence; it does not certify the realism of a rendered person.

This ledger separates evidence actually reviewed from the work needed to meet the requested breadth. It is **not a claim that each listed place or people has been individually researched**. Regional buckets below organize searches and representation review; they are not genetic boundaries. A community can span borders and appear in multiple places.

Status meanings:

- **Trait evidence:** a reviewed study directly measures at least one requested trait in a specified sample. It does not establish all traits or all inhabitants.
- **Genomic context:** reviewed sequencing/population history; no verified appearance distribution for the intended editor.
- **Inventory only:** a name/location is documented; trait-specific literature review is still pending.
- **Gap:** no adequate direct source was verified in this pass. This is not a claim that no source exists.

### Separate dimensions of coverage

| Dimension | Required evidence | Current boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Geographic/community | Actual participant location, self-description and sampling dates | Regional buckets and administrative entries alone are insufficient. |
| Feature | Measurements of the particular feature | Skin pigmentation does not establish hair, eyelids, teeth or beard distributions. |
| Life stage | Age distribution and relevant sampling context | Young-adult/child studies do not calibrate older adult faces. |
| Appearance combinations | Joint observations or consented reference examples | Do not generate a purported population by independently sampling marginal averages. |
| Geometry/materials/motion | Reference-matched stills, relighting and video | Genomes and landmark tables are not textured, rigged head assets. |
| Reuse | Applicable permissions for the actual data/asset | Open articles and publication consent do not automatically grant an avatar-asset licence. |

Further reference selection must include adult age variation, facial fat distribution, asymmetry, hair loss and grooming, facial hair on different adult faces, scars/restorations and habitual expression. These are appearance dimensions, not reasons to force identity labels or biological diagnoses into the editor. Within each available region, seek contrasting examples rather than one nominally representative person. No per-community numerical ranges are authorised by this ledger.

## Evidence card required for each future community or region

Each card must contain:

1. Self-designation, publication labels/aliases, geography and source date. Do not equate similar names without evidence.
2. Per-feature status for scalp hair, face, eyes, nose, brows, lids, lashes, lips, mouth, teeth, ears, beard, mustache and skin detail.
3. Exact study/sample, recruitment, age distribution, relevant covariates and measurement protocol. Distinguish genealogy, self-identification and genomic similarity.
4. Observed distributions and uncertainty where actually published. An average alone is insufficient for a representative generator.
5. Scope and contradictions: historical versus contemporary, community versus convenience sample, reference-panel overlap, and incompatible landmarks.
6. Asset/data rights and, where relevant, community authority for reuse. CARE provides a framework, not a substitute for the community's own rules [S36].
7. Product decision: background context, a measurement reference, a licensed asset, or no numerical use. Record author and review date.

Remaining research should prioritize modern Tamil Nadu reference coverage, underrepresented global regions, eyelid/lash and mustache distributions, adult/older-adult variation and valid asset permissions. Filling cells with invented values would not satisfy the request.
