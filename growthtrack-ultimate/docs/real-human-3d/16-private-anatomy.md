Updated 2026-10-23. This document contains the current approval plans for a **lifelike generic adult human** and the implementation record for its private anatomy in **Physique → 3D → Anatomy**. The first complete candidate uses the current adult male base. It consolidates the former anatomy asset plan, customization research plan and male anatomy research notes, and connects the full-human work to the existing face, surface and asset specifications. The historical 75/100 planning ledger is unchanged.

The implemented renderer uses procedural external surfaces on the existing licensed body. It includes a recessed meatus, outer/inner foreskin with a folded rim, a stitched pelvic transition and an optional sac fit around two independently sized/positioned internal guides. Layered surfaces and guide fitting are implemented; photorealism, medical accuracy, universal collision safety and the finished authored asset remain open. Female anatomy is still unavailable and its body base stays covered.

## Review findings and corrections

The previous plan was useful for an anatomy continuation but insufficient for the confirmed full-human goal. This revision corrects the following issues:

1. **Scope:** a pelvic patch cannot improve the face, hair, hands, feet or gait. The final acceptance decision must cover the complete figure and each close-up region. Existing work in [face/whole-human review](13-face-studio-review.md) and [articulated surfaces](12-articulated-surface-architecture.md) is part of the dependency map, not optional future polish.
2. **Asset architecture:** retaining the six old GLBs as fallbacks is sensible, but forbidding new body topology would prevent a full sculpt. I2 therefore adds a new public core/hero pair and compatible private packages. Each LOD has its own verified binding. No compatibility with an old or custom body is assumed from its filename.
3. **Scientific claims:** circumference, height and a few shape sliders do not identify one unique human surface. [Current contour checks](15-model-measurements-and-clone-isolation.md) use fixed stature fractions, not validated anatomical waist/chest/hip landmarks. Small solver residuals are not proof of anatomical accuracy.
4. **Material format:** the old skin and GLB plans assigned cavity to the blue channel of an ORM texture. Standard glTF uses red occlusion, green roughness and blue metallic. Skin metallic stays zero; cavity detail must not occupy a channel a standard loader interprets as metal. See [Khronos material/export documentation](https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Blender-IO/blob/main/docs/blender_docs/scene_gltf2.rst).
5. **Skin colour:** Fitzpatrick phototype describes sun response; it is not a calibrated RGB palette or a one-to-one melanin assay. The existing preset/ITA table and absorption constants are artistic reference fixtures. A mean-normalized detail map also does not guarantee unchanged perceived colour after lighting and tone mapping. These claims are corrected in [skin planning](04-skin-hair-eyes.md), informed by [DermNet](https://dermnetnz.org/topics/skin-phototype) and [Three.js colour management](https://threejs.org/manual/pages/color-management.html).
6. **Asset evidence:** bone counts, named expression channels, a valid GLB and passing software tests are compatibility evidence. They cannot certify natural anatomy, deformation or likeness. Published rendering timings are not benchmarks for this application.
7. **Research completion:** an unresolved binding, contact or optical-calibration question stays open. Recording a limitation is useful but does not satisfy a critical implementation exit check. The old source survey is not proof that authoring/export experiments succeeded.

The current anatomy baseline is still `integrated-adult-tissue-v7`. The existing generic study shows a sleeve-like root, a regular bright retracted fold and simplified ventral/distal tissue. The cause of each rim discontinuity still needs isolated geometry/material tests. No new candidate images were produced in this review. Existing full-human reviews also report synthetic close-up facial appearance, approximate eye optics, simplified dental geometry and unvalidated contact. These are recorded observations, not newly measured errors.

## Research plan R2

The following twenty work items expand and replace R1. Each must produce a concrete decision or experiment record in this document, linked to its implementation milestone. **Sources checked** means the publication or official documentation was reviewed. **Validation pending** means no new prototype or measurement has established the proposed result.

### Body, references and fitting

1. **R2-01 — define the reference human and review setup.** Use a coherent generic adult reference brief: body proportions, neutral expression, hair, skin detail scale and private anatomy belong to the same character. Lock camera field of view, distance, light direction, exposure and output transform for comparisons. Include clay, neutral skin and normal intended-use lighting. Deliverable: a reproducible view/lighting manifest and a feature checklist; no favourable single view can pass the figure.
2. **R2-02 — anatomical landmarks and measurement meaning.** Replace stature-fraction slices with authored landmarks and documented section planes where the UI claims an anatomical measure. Record posture, breathing state and measurement method. [NHANES anthropometry procedures](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/data/nhanes/public/2021/manuals/2021-Anthropometry-Procedures-Manual-508.pdf) illustrate why landmark and tape placement matter. Deliverable: a requested-versus-achieved report that separates source measurement uncertainty, landmark mismatch and numerical solver error. Do not force all three into a 1 mm accuracy claim.
3. **R2-03 — compare foundations and pin actual packages.** Keep the pinned MakeHuman foundation as the recommended starting point; evaluate Anny as an optional build-time source of proportions/rigging. Its current [official repository](https://github.com/naver/anny) distinguishes a 104-bone default from the 163-bone MakeHuman rig and warns that pose conventions differ. [MHR](https://github.com/facebookresearch/MHR) is a comparison source for LOD/corrective design; its nonlinear correctives are not automatically portable to glTF. Deliverable: pinned source/version, coordinate and channel mapping, and per-file provenance. More bones do not imply greater realism.
4. **R2-04 — separate reusable body data from research models.** [SMPL](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/) and [SMPL-X](https://smpl-x.is.tue.mpg.de/) inform shape/pose corrective design; they are not automatic runtime dependencies. The [SMPL-Body licence](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/license.html) covers a defined exported-body subset, while the [parametric model](https://smpl.is.tue.mpg.de/modellicense.html) and [SMPL-X model](https://smpl-x.is.tue.mpg.de/modellicense.html) have different terms. Deliverable: package-specific suitability, not a blanket claim that every related asset is either freely shippable or prohibited. No new model downloads or purchases are selected by this review.
5. **R2-05 — whole-body sculpt and topology.** Specify skull, ears, nostrils, neck, shoulders, torso, joints, hands and feet before adding pores. Preserve plausible soft-tissue distribution and individual asymmetry without prescribing a single idealized body. Deliverable: a neutral sculpt, silhouette/landmark checklist and deformation-aware topology brief. The candidate must be evaluated at full-body scale as well as face/hand/foot close crops.
6. **R2-06 — fitting and uncertainty.** Study which entered dimensions can be fitted independently and which compete under the available shape basis. Preserve original records, report residuals and request no hidden rewrite to resolve an inconsistent input set. Unspecified shape remains a generic choice. Deliverable: controlled synthetic fixtures with known geometry, followed by the candidate's landmark tests; passing a synthetic fixture does not establish person-specific biological truth.

### Face, eyes, hair and articulation

7. **R2-07 — facial anatomy and expression.** Author neutral facial form plus expressions that preserve lip contact, eyelid contact, cheek volume and jaw/dental alignment. [Apple's face coefficients](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/arkit/arfaceanchor) describe named feature motions; providing their names is not equivalent to authoring a realistic face or implementing the complete FACS system. Deliverable: neutral, blink, smile, jaw-open and speech-shape review clips, including combinations and return to neutral.
8. **R2-08 — eye geometry and optics.** Treat sclera, cornea, iris, pupil, tearline and lids as coupled structures. Test gaze, convergence, pupil changes and blink clearance rather than painting a shiny sphere. [Berard et al., 2014](https://studios.disneyresearch.com/2014/11/19/high-quality-capture-of-eyes/) demonstrates the importance of distinct eye components and individual shape. Deliverable: an optically plausible approximation with documented parameters, no claim of an ophthalmic simulation.
9. **R2-09 — mouth, ears, hands, feet and nails.** Review true nostril/ear depth, separate upper/lower teeth and gums, tongue attachment, finger pads, palm creases, knuckle folds, nail beds, toe spacing and the foot arch. Preserve genuine openings while preventing sight through the head. Deliverable: authored geometry and corrective/contact checks; the existing projected patches and simple dental assembly remain fallbacks until replaced.
10. **R2-10 — scalp, brow, lash and body hair.** Compare groomed strand/ribbon and card representations by silhouette, fibre direction, highlight response, alpha sorting and cost. [Marschner et al., 2003](https://aperture.stanford.edu/papers/hair/) provides a measured hair-scattering reference. Deliverable: one coherent groom with tier-specific representations. Brows/lashes must follow expression and blink; adult private hair stays within the existing visibility policy. Seeded procedural generation is repeatable, not proof of invisible tiling or plausible growth.

### Skin, lighting and deformation

11. **R2-11 — colour and reflectance separation.** Separate base pigment, regional variation and lighting; preserve meaningful low-frequency features instead of blindly removing them by blur division. [Digital Emily](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalEmily/) illustrates separating surface/specular and diffuse response during capture. Deliverable: a lawful generic map source and an end-to-end colour-space test. The capture method does not grant rights to redistribute the actor's scans or maps.
12. **R2-12 — subsurface response by tier.** Compare a measured-reference offline render, a bounded pre-integrated approximation and skin-masked separable scattering. [Jensen et al.](https://graphics.stanford.edu/~srm/publications/SG01-subsurf-abstract.html) and [Jimenez et al.](https://www.iryoku.com/separable-sss/) provide methods, not an accuracy or frame-time guarantee for this viewer. Deliverable: fixed-light comparisons and actual GPU timings, including boundaries between lips, eyes, hair, nails and private tissue. Keep emissive skin off; do not label transmission, clearcoat or an arbitrary thickness scalar as validated diffusion.
13. **R2-13 — detail that follows strain.** Determine where static normal maps suffice and where bending or expression needs wrinkle/roughness corrections. [Nagano et al., 2015](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/SkinStretch/) reports that stretched and compressed skin has different microstructure response. Deliverable: region-scaled detail with controlled wrinkle masks and anti-aliasing. Never stretch one pore pattern uniformly over the face, torso, palms and private tissue.
14. **R2-14 — body and joint correctives.** Define local axes, neutral pose, skinning method, joint pivots and a single transform order. Evaluate elbows, shoulders, wrists, knees, hips and neck for collapse, volume loss and twisting. Deliverable: explicit shape-dependent pivots and local correctives, not only additional skeleton nodes. Validate source convention changes before importing an alternative rig.

### Private anatomy, motion and contact

15. **R2-15 — connected private tissue and pelvic transition.** Produce a landmark brief for pubic/perineal continuity, shaft/glans/corona/aperture, ventral attachment, inner/outer prepuce and sac. [OpenStax](https://openstax.org/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e/pages/27-1-anatomy-and-physiology-of-the-testicular-reproductive-system), [foreskin](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23175-foreskin) and [frenulum](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/body/23533-penis-frenulum) references support the structural distinctions. Deliverable: an authored continuous private/pelvic patch, with a versioned seam into the actual body. Distinct render objects are not a welded mesh simply because their positions are close.
16. **R2-16 — variation and independent states.** Retain separately specified resting/erect geometry, coverage/retraction, both testicular records, external sac shaping and regional pigment. The sac should read as soft tissue around contents rather than two rigid balls or a prescribed deep cleft. Deliverable: shape/contact fixtures including asymmetry and relaxed/contracted presentation. Appearance, age, ethnicity or one endpoint must not supply another person's intimate measurements.
17. **R2-17 — motion and contact.** Combine breathing, gaze, blink, weight shifts, foot planting and restrained secondary motion without visible cycles, drift or perpetual movement. Test body/cloth, hand/body, lid/eye and private tissue contacts on the actual rendered surfaces. [IPC](https://ipc-sim.github.io/) demonstrates that robust contact requires a defined method; its guarantees cannot be borrowed by a heuristic. Deliverable: a declared pose/transition set and failure report. No full continuum tissue solver or universal collision claim is assumed.

### Delivery and acceptance

18. **R2-18 — export and deformation transfer.** Establish a round trip from Blender to glTF to the installed renderer, including normals, tangents, shape keys, joint conventions and authored sculpt offsets. [glTF](https://registry.khronos.org/glTF/specs/2.0/glTF-2.0.html) describes supported data; [Blender cage binding](https://docs.blender.org/manual/en/latest/modeling/modifiers/deform/mesh_deform.html) does not export a working browser modifier by itself. Deliverable: a tested native-asset adapter and per-LOD binding, with no double skinning or accidental procedural reconstruction of the sculpt.
19. **R2-19 — streaming, LODs and memory.** Separate a public core/hero pair from adult-revealed private packages. A new public core must actually omit embedded private accessors; hiding the existing mesh does not meet that network-loading contract. Evaluate geometry, morph and texture compression with local decoders, masks and normals intact. Deliverable: measured first paint, reveal latency, GPU memory, p95 frame cost and failure fallback on named desktop/mobile devices. The old compressed-size budgets are targets, not already satisfied facts.
20. **R2-20 — human acceptance and honest completion.** Review the same generic character in clay, neutral materials and skin, under fixed light and view controls, both still and moving. Record per-region results for silhouette, optical/material response, expression, contact and display stability; include independent human visual review before claiming lifelike acceptance. Deliverable: matched baseline/candidate evidence and a list of passes, failures and unknowns. Test counts, GLB conformance and subjective review remain separate evidence types; no aggregate realism percentage is assigned.

### Research status and exit criteria

This review expands the source survey and corrects the scope/material/licence assumptions. The existing software and screenshot evidence is historical. No new sculpt, optical calibration, rig import, source-data download, binding/export spike, physical-device benchmark or human visual acceptance review has been completed here. Blender 5.1.1 availability was checked in the preceding research turn; that alone is not an exporter test.

R2 is decision-ready when every critical choice above has a cited rationale, artifact specification and validation method. A feasibility experiment closes only when its measured result passes its declared check. Known unsupported cases remain visible; they do not pass by being documented. Approving R2 accepts this research programme and the generic-adult target. Implementation still requires approval of I2.

## Implementation plan I2

**Architecture:** build one coherent new human asset family from the pinned foundation, with public core/hero bodies and compatible adult private packages. Use authored geometry, independent shape records, material maps and local pose correctives. Retain the six existing GLBs and v7 as compatible fallbacks. The selected new body can have new topology; its landmark/measurement and attachment adapters must be explicit.

### Work sequence and deliverables

1. **M0 — reference, evidence and contracts (R2-01–06, 18–20).** Lock the generic character brief, source versions, units, handedness, neutral pose, landmark definitions and review camera/lights. Inventory existing runtime gaps from the face, body and anatomy reviews. Build isolated binding/export/material probes only after the two approvals. Exit: measured feasibility results and compatible manifest contracts. Anny is an optional build-time comparison, not an unpinned runtime or machine-learning dependency.
2. **M1 — whole-body neutral sculpt and new asset family (R2-05, 09, 14, 19).** Author body proportions, head/ears/nose, hands/feet and major soft tissue on one coherent neutral adult. Retopologize for movement and map every required landmark. Create public core and hero exports with genuine geometry LODs. Exit: full-body and regional clay reviews pass; the public files have no private geometry payload. Keep the old six binaries unchanged.
3. **M2 — measurements, shape and rig adaptation (R2-02, 06, 14, 18).** Transfer or reauthor the existing body controls onto the new topology, including shape-dependent joint pivots and independent Current/Goal buffers. Measure actual landmark sections and report unsupported or conflicting requested values. Exit: unit fixtures, native-mesh fitting reports and pose/clone isolation pass. Numerical fitting targets apply to declared measurable geometry, not an unobserved real person's surface.
4. **M3 — face, eyes and oral assembly (R2-07–09).** Author facial expressions, lids/tearline/cornea/iris, upper/lower teeth, gums and tongue; connect all parts to the new rig/shape system. Add local corrective and wrinkle controls. Exit: neutral, blink/gaze, smile, jaw-open and speech clips remain coherent through face/body shape changes, with no lip/eye penetrations, black mouth leaks or detached accessories. Shape names alone do not pass.
5. **M4 — skin and optical pipeline (R2-11–13).** Build region-scaled albedo variation, normal, roughness, thickness and optional feature masks. Use standard ORM channels, linear/non-colour data maps and one correct display conversion. Test inherited tones and private/body transitions under the same illumination; preserve intentional warm/cool undertones. Add the tier-appropriate scattering method only if its quality/cost comparison passes. Exit: no systematic unintended cast, waxy surface, bright seams or lighting baked into albedo; normal-map-only detail is not accepted as silhouette geometry.
6. **M5 — coherent hair and small surface parts (R2-09–10, 13).** Author scalp, brow/lash and optional facial/body hair, nail beds and regional skin detail. Build strand/ribbon or card LODs from one groom. Exit: correct growth direction/silhouette, expression following, robust transparency and no flickering or obvious repeated tiling at the declared review distance. A procedurally generated texture still requires visual inspection.
7. **M6 — native private anatomy and tissue states (R2-15–16, 18).** Author the integrated pelvic/private surface, aperture wall, ventral connection, layered prepuce and soft sac. Bind it to the specific new public-body boundaries. Reuse the centimetre/state solver as a control mechanism through a tested adapter; native authored assets bypass `createAnatomyTissues()`'s legacy ring reconstruction. Exit: remove the visible collar, rigid fold band, pinched terminal shape and unmatched tissue shading in the supported state/shape fixtures. No declared compatibility with a legacy or custom body without its own verified mapping.
8. **M7 — motion, corrective poses and contacts (R2-14, 17).** Integrate idle/gaze/blink/breathing, stance/gait, foot lock and restrained secondary motion with reduced-motion and capture freezes. Validate non-adjacent contacts and declared movement transitions, excluding only known topological attachments. The body boundary receives the actual posed anchors in one coordinate frame, with transforms applied once. Exit: no detected penetrations, inverted faces, sliding feet or detached parts in the named test set; residual failures remain explicitly unsupported.
9. **M8 — streaming, compression and quality tiers (R2-18–19).** Package core/hero and private full/lite assets with per-LOD morph/binding data, source hashes and local decoder/transcoder resources. Validate before/after quantization and mipmapping. Exercise cancelled loads, covered reload, stale requests, reveal/hide, quality changes and failed decode. Exit: no anatomy fetch before eligibility/reveal in the new family; intact covered-body fallback on failure; measured budget compliance and successful named-device checks.
10. **M9 — full-human acceptance and handoff (R2-20).** Run relevant unit, persistence, capture, export and browser checks, then review matched stills and motion clips for every region. Deliver editable source, pinned build recipe, provenance, model assets, evidence and one verified archive. Promote the new family only after every declared critical gate passes. A passing anatomy patch cannot close the whole-human milestone while face, hair, hands, feet or motion remain visibly unfinished.

These are internal exit checks within the approved implementation, not extra approval questions at each step. Continue autonomously through the approved scope. A materially different source/architecture or a requested person-specific likeness needs a revised plan. No publishing, paid asset purchase or owner-data migration is included.

### Compatibility, failure and privacy contracts

The manifest declares the body/rig version, public-body hashes, private-patch version, boundary correspondences, landmark names, per-LOD bindings, corrective names, map channels and codec versions. Preserve saved measurement values and source state. New internal landmark metadata can live in assets; any persistence change must have an explicit backward-compatible schema design before use. Existing people, profile data and shared caches must never share mutable rig/geometry buffers between clones.

The new adapter must return a clearly defined pre-skin or post-skin space and apply skinning exactly once. Authored detail offsets must rotate/scale with their deformation frame. A seam needs position, normal/tangent, UV/material and deformation compatibility; near-coincident vertices alone are insufficient. Capture and public export use the intact public body and omit all private surfaces, guides and metadata. One sensitive parent owns private visibility and cleanup. A cancelled reveal cannot let a late request expose the asset.

### Acceptance matrix and budget policy

- **Character and geometry:** review front, back, side, both obliques, underside and regional head/eye/mouth/hand/foot/private crops. Include neutral and several independent body shapes and asymmetries without using ethnicity as a deterministic shape or pigment input. The initial native private candidate uses length/girth engineering fixtures of 9.2/9.3 cm, 6/12 cm and 16/8 cm in each separately specified state; they are not clinical ranges.
- **Deformation:** retraction 0/25/50/75/100, bends in both directions, independent guide placement, height scales 0.85/1.15, declared body-mass/pelvis morph endpoints, arm/hand expressions and hip flexion 45/90 degrees, abduction 15/30 degrees and seated poses. Verify rig axes first. Test transitions as well as endpoints; name untested combinations.
- **Materials and perception:** clay and surface-detail-disabled views precede skin acceptance. Use fixed neutral/diffuse/grazing lights, deliberately warm/cool environment checks, at least three separated inherited skin colours, and a no-light sanity check with background/environment illumination also controlled. Compare colour swatches before photographic matching. Do not interpret naturally warm undertones as an automatic failure.
- **Numerical evidence:** exact mapped seams target 0.1 mm residual in common posed space; compression adds at most 0.1 mm landmark error. The greater of 1 mm or 1% remains a proposed neutral solver target for declared fitted dimensions, with separate landmark/source-measurement uncertainty and post-pose residuals. None is a medical accuracy standard. Actual rendered-surface contact checks remain distinct from the legacy 52-sample cage readout.
- **Delivery budgets:** retain [the whole-human tier targets](07-asset-pipeline-and-glb-spec.md): first paint at most 5 MB, mobile core at most 3 MB, GPU textures at most 40/100/150 MiB for LOW/BALANCED/HIGH, total p95 frame time at most 16.7 ms desktop / 33 ms phone. The private package proposes at most 20,000/8,000 triangles, 5/2 MB compressed payload and 24/6 MiB additional texture allocation for full/lite, always within the total tier budget. Measure the exact device/viewport/driver/browser and cold/warm conditions. These are proposed acceptance ceilings, not existing achievements or performance copied from a paper.

Perceptual and automated checks must both pass. A named reviewer should compare the complete figure and each critical region with the reference brief, recording specific defects rather than assigning a realism percentage. If independent human visual review or physical-device evidence is unavailable, that gate stays pending; do not claim the full human complete. Lower tiers may reduce detail but must retain correct shape, essential anatomy and privacy behavior.

## Data, measurements and controls

The existing `anatomyV2` record stores `schemaVersion: 2`, independent `current` and `goal` profiles, and a cm/in display preference. Each profile contains:

- `penis.resting` and `penis.erect`: optional measurements, selected section input mode, measurement method and shape settings; `stretchedRecord` remains separate.
- `scrotum`: optional external envelope dimensions/shape, independent left/right testicular records, relaxed/contracted presentation, `fitMode`, `paddingCm` and reversible `mirrorPlacement`. Each side can retain separate `placement` offsets/tilt alongside its measurements.
- `buttocks`: optional measurements and posterior contour settings.
- `skinRegions`: inherited or overridden pigment, tan intensity/edge and surface detail for groin, shaft, glans, scrotum, perineum and either buttock.

Persist linear measurements in cm and volume records in mL. Null means unspecified. A generic fallback draws a preview while the original record stays null. Legacy resting length/girth seed resting fields only; old units and unspecified methods retain their original meaning. Nested edits merge through the existing profile/API flow, preserving unrelated settings, the other state and the other Current/Goal profile. An appearance setting must not rewrite dimensions.

Length is fitted as the visible-junction centerline path. Visible straight distance, curved centerline and bone-pressed records have different roots or paths. The editor preserves a bone-pressed record and explains that the renderer cannot reproduce its hidden root. The generated readout is explicitly before body pose.

Circumference is a closed local section perimeter. Width and depth are independent diameters. The selected mode chooses circumference plus ovalness, or width plus depth, while retaining all supplied records. Base, middle and distal records remain distinct. Curvature, orientation, taper, glans length/flare, corona/frenulum definition and foreskin coverage/retraction are shape controls, rather than accuracy percentages.

External sac height/width/depth, neck width, hang, asymmetry and folds change its envelope independently of shaft girth. Optional internal dimensions/volume produce hidden-by-default schematic guides. **Fit sac around guides** explicitly lets them expand the external envelope, retaining the entered sac dimensions as a minimum starting shape. **Use sac dimensions** retains the earlier envelope behavior. Neither mode establishes clinical volume. Buttock breadth, projection, fullness, asymmetry, cleft, hip dip and thigh transition affect the existing posterior envelope; exact girth/volume locking remains unvalidated.

The editor reports optional recorded-field completeness for the displayed profile/state. This count describes saved entries only. The separate 0–100 appearance-detail control changes material detail and has no meaning as a percentage of anatomical completion or likeness.
