“Base circumference” needs context. It is a full loop at a specific attachment-end section of a named structure. Choose the local-section guide to see base, middle and distal loops. Width is a straight distance across; circumference goes all the way around.

One tape loop cannot define a 3D body.

Change the oval shape while preserving its approximate circumference. The same tape reading can describe a wider, shallower section or a narrower, deeper one.

Different dimensions, different jobs

Measure size. Edit distribution.

Girth + ovalness, or width + depth

Pick one input mode. Derive the remaining values from the fitted mesh. A circle is only one possible cross-section.

Taper, fullness and projection

Taper changes section size along a region. Fullness changes where it bulges. Projection measures extension from a defined plane. These need independent shape controls.

Curvature versus orientation

Curvature bends a contour or centerline. Orientation turns the whole part. Specify the plane, endpoints and state before presenting an angle.

Volume needs a boundary

A closed outer envelope can have a geometric volume. It cannot tell us muscle or fat mass. Buttock volume needs a documented anterior cap and shared regional borders.

Unknown stays unknown

A generic preview is not a measurement. Fit only supported dimensions; show conflicts and residual errors without changing the original record.

Skin tone, local pigment and tan are separate.

Try the layers independently. A naturally darker region does not automatically mean tanning. Surface texture and light response need additional renderer work.

Color is only one layer

Albedo carries color without painted-in lighting. Roughness controls reflections; normal detail adds pores and folds; subsurface scattering gives skin a softer light response.

Phototype is not a color picker

Fitzpatrick types describe response to sunlight. Use a continuous color editor and independent region masks for appearance.

Neutral lighting helps comparisons

Keep exposure and lighting fixed while editing. Check under a second light afterward. Phone photos include lighting, white balance and camera processing.

Build realism in layers. Verify each one.

“70%” and “100%” are subjective descriptions. Track shape accuracy, personal likeness, material quality, deformation and usability separately.

New data that matters

Pair girths with width/depth and named sections. Add silhouette proportions, tissue distribution, local contours, bilateral variation and face detail only where the mesh supports them.

Keep weight/composition as uncertain context. A tape cannot measure regional tissue volume, and more input fields cannot repair missing anatomy or materials.

What a field must show

A diagram, simple definition, tool, pose/state, exact landmarks, common mistakes, units, preview effect, provenance and support status. Include “Skip” and a separate visual-adjustment route.

First implementation: height, protocol-specific waist, hips, shoulder breadth, chest depth, and base/middle/distal local sections.

Research behind the plan

  • TU Delft / DINED — anthropometric dimensionsDistinct dimensions and measurement vocabulary; app-specific section definitions still need documentation.
  • CDC / NHANES — anthropometry protocol · WHO — waist circumferenceMeasurement sites differ between protocols. Do not silently merge them or reuse them for a different formula.
  • SMPL-X — body, articulated hands and facial expressionResearch reference for integrated shape/pose; not a complete photorealistic avatar. Licensing needs separate review.
  • NVIDIA — digital human rendering workflowSeparate skin, eyes, mouth and hair, with surface maps and subsurface scattering. Browser implementation must fit its own budget.
  • DermNet — skin phototype · Three.js — color managementSeparate sun response from appearance, and handle display colors and linear rendering data correctly.
