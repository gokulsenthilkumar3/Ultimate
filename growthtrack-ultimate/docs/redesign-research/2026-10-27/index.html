Acceptance that goes beyond fitting

  • Laptop: 1280×720 and 1366×768.
  • Desktop: 1440×900 and 1920×1080.
  • Real zoom, narrow viewports, keyboard and trackpad.
  • Empty, populated, loading, failure, retry and save recovery.
  • Current/Goal isolation, person switching and protected exports.
  • Light, dark, forced colors and reduced motion.

Keep the existing contracts

Preserve routes, records, units, finance arithmetic, Notes revisions, drafts, timers, ownership and capability states.

New geometry, rigs, licensed assets, schema migrations, integration completion and deployment require their own scope. Layout polish does not certify human realism.
