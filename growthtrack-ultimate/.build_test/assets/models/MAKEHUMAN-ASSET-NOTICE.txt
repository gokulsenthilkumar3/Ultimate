The high-density body source used by humanoid-base.glb is derived from the
MakeHuman 1.1.1 bundled HM08 base mesh and target data.

MakeHuman bundled assets are released under CC0 1.0 Universal. The source
package is not shipped to the browser; it is used by scripts/build-makehuman-
glb.py to generate the optimized runtime asset.

Source project: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman
License: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md

Surface maps: SkinNormal_GeometryBaked and SkinAO_Roughness_GeometryBaked are
generated offline from the embedded GrowthTrackBody UVs and triangle topology
by scripts/bake-geometry-surface.mjs. The AO contribution is also baked into
the three skin albedo variants because the runtime body has no UV2 channel.
